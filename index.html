Exness Login is the authentication gateway used to access the Exness Personal Area and connected trading terminals (such as the Exness Terminal, Exness Trade mobile app, MetaTrader 4, and MetaTrader 5). Through this portal, clients manage their profile, verify their identity, deposit or withdraw capital, and execute trades across global financial markets.

Under RBI and FEMA regulations, resident Indians are prohibited from remitting or depositing funds for leveraged retail forex/CFD trading on unauthorized overseas platforms, and doing so carries legal and financial penalties.
The comprehensive guide below explains how the Exness login architecture and platform features operate in jurisdictions where the service is legally permitted.

1. The Two-Tier Exness Login Architecture

Just like several other multi-asset CFD brokers, Exness separates account administration from live market execution. Understanding the difference between your two sets of credentials prevents the vast majority of login errors.

A. Personal Area (PA) Login — Master Account

Your Personal Area is the central administrative hub for your entire relationship with Exness.
• Login ID: Your registered Email Address.
• Password: The master password created when registering your profile.
• Primary Uses: Completing identity and address verification (KYC), opening new real or demo trading accounts, depositing and withdrawing funds, adjusting leverage limits, viewing transaction histories, and accessing analytics.

B. Trading Account Login — Terminal Access

Every individual trading account you create inside your Personal Area—whether it is a Standard account, Pro account, Raw Spread account, Zero account, or Demo account—generates its own unique terminal credentials:
• Trading Account Number (MT4/MT5 Login): A fixed numeric ID displayed on your account card inside the Personal Area.
• Trading Password: The specific password you set for that individual trading account (distinct from your Personal Area password).
• Read-Only Access (Investor Password): A secondary view-only password that lets you share real-time account performance with mentors, investors, or tracking tools without allowing anyone to place or close trades.
• Server Name: The exact server cluster assigned to that account (for example, Exness-MT5Real14 or Exness-MT5Trial7), which is visible under Account Information in your Personal Area.

2. Step-by-Step: How to Log In to Exness (Where Permitted)

Depending on your device and preferred trading interface, you can log in using four primary methods.

Method 1: Logging into the Web Personal Area

Use a desktop or mobile web browser to access your main account dashboard.
1. Navigate to the Official Portal: Open a secure browser and go directly to the official Exness Login Page (my.exness.com). Always verify the exact URL and SSL padlock to protect against lookalike phishing domains.
2. Enter Your Email Address: Type the exact email address you used during registration.
3. Enter Your Master Password: Type your Personal Area password (watch for Caps Lock or extra spaces).
4. Complete Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): If prompted, enter the verification code sent to your registered phone number, email, or authenticator app.
5. Click Sign In: You will land directly on the My Accounts dashboard inside your Personal Area.

Method 2: Logging into the Exness Trade Mobile App

The Exness Trade app combines Personal Area management and mobile order execution into a single application.
1. Download the Official App: Install Exness Trade from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, verifying that the publisher is Exness.
2. Tap Sign In: Open the app and choose Sign In (rather than Register).
3. Input Personal Area Credentials: Enter your registered email address and master password.
4. Configure Passcode & Biometrics: Set up a 6-digit app passcode and enable Face ID or fingerprint authentication so subsequent logins take seconds without retyping your password.
5. Choose Built-In Terminal Preference: Under Profile > User Settings > Trading Terminal, you can choose whether to trade using the native Exness Terminal or the Built-in MetaTrader 5 interface directly inside the app.

Method 3: Logging into the Exness Web Terminal

The Exness Terminal is a browser-based trading platform featuring TradingView charting tools, over 100 technical indicators, and one-click trading without requiring software downloads.
1. Log in to your Web Personal Area using Method 1.
2. Locate the specific Real or Demo account card under My Accounts.
3. Click the Trade button next to that account and select Exness Terminal (or open it from the top-right grid menu).
4. The terminal launches in a new browser tab automatically authenticated for that trading account—no separate server name or numeric login is required.

Method 4: Logging into MetaTrader 4 (MT4) or MetaTrader 5 (MT5)

Traders who rely on custom indicators, multi-monitor setups, or automated Expert Advisors (EAs) frequently connect their Exness accounts to standalone MT4 or MT5 applications.
1. Retrieve Your Terminal Details First: Log in to your Exness Personal Area, click the gear icon (or dropdown arrow) on your chosen trading account card, and select Account Information. Note your numeric MT4/MT5 Login and exact Server name.
2. Open MetaTrader: Launch MT4 or MT5 on your Windows PC, Mac, iOS, or Android device.
3. Select Login to Existing Account:
• Desktop: Click File > Login to Trade Account (or Open an Account).
• Mobile: Tap Manage Accounts (or Settings > New Account), tap the + icon, and select Login to an existing account.
4. Search for the Broker Server: Type Exness Technologies Ltd into the broker search bar, select it, and choose the exact server name listed in your Account Information (e.g., Exness-MT5Real or Exness-MT5Trial).
5. Enter Terminal Credentials: Input your numeric Trading Account Number as the Login and your Trading Password (not your email or Personal Area password).
6. Confirm Connection: Click Finish or Sign In. Live price ticks and a green connection bar in the bottom-right corner (on desktop) confirm that you are connected.

3. How to Use the Exness Login Portal Effectively

Once logged in, navigating the platform follows a structured workflow from compliance setup to order management.

Step 1: Complete Account Verification (KYC)

Unverified accounts face strict deposit and trading restrictions. Inside the Personal Area:
• Complete your Economic Profile questionnaire (covering trading experience and financial background).
• Upload a clear, valid Proof of Identity (Passport, National ID card, or Driver's License).
• Upload a valid Proof of Residence (utility bill, bank statement, or government tax document dated within the last 6 months showing your full name and residential address).

Step 2: Open and Customize Trading Accounts

Inside My Accounts, click Open New Account to tailor accounts to your strategy:
• Demo vs. Real: Always test strategies and order sizing on a Demo account loaded with virtual funds before committing real capital.
• Account Tiers: Choose between Standard / Standard Cent (zero commission, spread-only pricing suitable for smaller balances or beginners) and Professional accounts (Pro, Raw Spread, or Zero, which offer tighter spreads with either zero or fixed per-lot commissions).
• Account Currency & Leverage: Select your base account currency (note that an account's base currency cannot be changed once created) and configure your maximum leverage ratio. Higher leverage reduces required margin per trade but substantially increases the risk of rapid account loss.

Step 3: Deposit, Transfer, and Withdraw Funds

All funding operations happen inside the Personal Area rather than inside MetaTrader:
• Deposits: Select Deposit from the left-hand menu, choose a payment method available in your jurisdiction (bank transfer, cards, e-wallets, or approved digital payment rails), pick the target trading account number, and follow the confirmation prompts.
• Internal Transfers: Use the Transfer tab to move funds instantaneously between your own Exness trading accounts, even if they use different base currencies (automatic conversion applies).
• Withdrawals: Select Withdrawal from the main menu. Anti-money laundering rules require withdrawals to be routed back to the exact payment method and account holder name used for the original deposit.

Step 4: Execute and Manage Trades

Whether you trade via the Exness Terminal, Exness Trade App, or MT4/MT5:
1. Select an Asset Class: Choose from Forex pairs (e.g., EUR/USD), Precious Metals (e.g., XAU/USD), Energies (e.g., USOIL), Indices (e.g., US30), or Stock CFDs.
2. Choose Execution Type: Select Market execution to enter immediately at the live bid/ask price, or Pending (Limit/Stop) to trigger an order automatically if price reaches a predetermined level.
3. Define Lot Size: Set your volume carefully (e.g., 0.01 micro-lot) relative to your total equity so a single adverse price swing does not wipe out your balance.
4. Set Stop Loss (SL) and Take Profit (TP): Always attach a Stop Loss to cap downside risk automatically and a Take Profit to lock in gains at your target price.
5. Monitor Margin & Equity: Track your Balance, Equity, Free Margin, and Margin Level % in real time. While Exness features a 0% stop-out level on many account types and negative balance protection, leveraged CFD positions can still lose 100% of deposited equity during high volatility.

Step 5: Use Built-In Analytical & Account Tools

• Analytics & Economic Calendar: Inside the Personal Area, access technical analysis views, market news, and high-impact economic event schedules to anticipate volatility spikes.
• Performance & Transaction History: Review closed order metrics, win/loss ratios, and downloadable statements under Performance and Transaction History.
• Archive Management: Inactive trading accounts automatically move to the Archived tab after a period of inactivity and can be restored anytime by clicking Reactivate.

4. Troubleshooting Common Exness Login Issues


Problem / Error Typical Cause Solution
"Authorization Failed" / "Invalid Account" on MT4/MT5 Typing your email address instead of the numeric MT4/MT5 login ID, using your Personal Area password instead of your Trading password, or picking the wrong server. Open Personal Area > My Accounts > Gear Icon > Account Information to verify the exact numeric ID and server name, or click Change Trading Password to reset it.
Forgot Personal Area Password Entering an outdated password on my.exness.com. Click I forgot my password on the sign-in screen, enter your registered email address, and verify via the security code sent to your phone or email.
Security Code (2FA) Not Received Telecom SMS delays or network congestion. Wait 60 seconds and request a resend, verify your mobile signal, or switch your security type in Personal Area settings to an authenticator app (TOTP) for offline code generation.
Trade Button Disabled / Market Closed Attempting to place orders during weekend market closures or logging in with a read-only Investor password. Check market trading hours for the specific asset, and confirm you logged into MT4/MT5 using your main Trading Password rather than your read-only password.
Website or App Blocked / Restricted Accessing from a restricted jurisdiction (such as the US, UK, EU, or regions with local regulatory blocks). Respect local financial laws; do not use VPNs or false residency documents to bypass regional restrictions, as doing so violates Terms of Service and risks frozen funds.


5. Security & Risk Best Practices

1. Bookmark Official Domains: Scammers routinely clone brokerage login pages to harvest credentials. Bookmark my.exness.com and verify the domain before entering your password.
2. Use Strong, Unique Passwords: Never reuse passwords across your email account and financial portals. If someone compromises your email, they can reset your brokerage password.
3. Never Share Your Master or Trading Passwords: Legitimate support staff will never ask for your password. Only share your read-only Investor Password if you want a third party to audit your trading performance.
4. Understand Leverage Risk: Contracts for Difference (CFDs) are complex, high-risk derivatives. High leverage amplifies both gains and losses rapidly, and you should never trade with borrowed funds or money essential for living expenses.
